Social Responsibilities of Art Museums

January – April 2025

Collaborators: Samantha Barnes, Ashley Villegas Hernandez, JaDea Houston, Cassandra (Cassie) Hughes.

  • I selected three books and wrote an exhibition statement about Edges of Ailey at the Whitney Museum of American Art.
  • Books:
    • Alvin Ailey and A. Peter Bailey, Revelations: The Autobiography of Alvin Ailey (1995)
    • Andrew Eccles, Ailey Ascending: A Portrait in Motion (2008)

Senior Seminar Exhibition

August - September 2025

Collaborators: Samantha Barnes, Ashley Villegas Hernandez, Ben Wuoristo, Lisa Yen.

  • This was my graduating class's senior project at FIT. We had to ideate, plan, and execute an exhibit for a display case in the Feldman Building's lobby.
  • Our theme was "The Artist's Perspective." We split up into five groups, each one covering a different topic related to the overall theme.
  • My group's section was called "Art and Activism: LGBTQ Visual Resistance." We chose a variety of objects made by LGBTQ artists, from buttons to posters.
  • My role was registrar. I kept track of objects borrowed and recorded them in an exhibition checklist. I also kept track of each artist/organization's contact information.
